# Toy fMRI study of auditory word recognition

## Abstract
We examined auditory word recognition with functional magnetic resonance imaging.

## Methods
### Participants
Twenty-four healthy adults (12 female; age 19-32 years) provided written consent.

### Experimental task
Participants heard spoken real words and pronounceable pseudowords. They pressed the
left button for a real word and the right button for a pseudoword. The task was
performed in the scanner in four event-related runs.

### MRI acquisition and analysis
Images were acquired on a 3 T Siemens scanner. Whole-brain BOLD responses were
modelled with a general linear model. The main contrast was real words greater than
pseudowords.

## Results
Real words greater than pseudowords activated left inferior frontal and temporal cortex.

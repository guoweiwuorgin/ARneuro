# Toy EEG study of visuomotor attention

## Methods
### Participants
Eighteen adults completed the study (9 female; age 20-30 years).

### Task and recording
Participants viewed coloured shapes and pressed a button when the target shape appeared.
EEG was recorded with a 64-channel cap. The task was completed outside the MRI scanner.

## Results
Target trials produced a larger P300 response than non-target trials.
